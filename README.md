* [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) - Developer-first financial infrastructure supporting global credit card processing.

## 🏛️ US Entity Formation & Legal Compliance

Essential compliance frameworks for non-resident founders accessing US payment rails and banking:

* [US LLC Guide Hub](https://kuajingbase.com/en/legal/us-llc) - Master directory of statutory state costs, EIN processing channels, and tax calendars.
* **State-by-State Formation Walkthroughs**:
  * [Michigan LLC Guide](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/4g01kjlq-michigan-llc-registration-50-filing-fee-full-guide-for-non) - The $50 filing fee lean structure ($25 annual statement).
  * [Wyoming LLC Guide](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/sq93bu1u-wyoming-llc-registration-cost-privacy-rules-no-state-income) - Industry benchmark for privacy protection and zero state income tax.
  * [Delaware LLC Cost Audit](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/o9jguzyy-delaware-llc-registration-the-real-cost-when-its-not-worth) - Understanding the $400 annual franchise tax before filing.
  * [Florida LLC Guide](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/m0yuow5o-florida-llc-registration-cost-complete-fee-breakdown-for) - Commercial breakdown and the strict May 1st late fee rule.
* **Federal IRS Compliance**:
  * [IRS Form 5472 & 1120 Walkthrough](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/5wsxhjzi-how-to-file-form-5472-and-1120-complete-tax-guide-for) - Mandatory annual informational reporting to prevent the $25,000 failure-to-file penalty.
  * [IRS EIN Application Protocol](https://kuajingbase.com/en/articles/i8r25f67-irs-ein-application-for-non-us-residents-step-by-step-2026) - Phone and fax filing channels without a US SSN.
